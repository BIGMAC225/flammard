# How to run an L10 on the site

The weekly Level 10 meeting (Tuesdays, 10:00 a.m. Central) runs from the
meeting page, EOS Sections tab. The lists carry over from week to week: the
site never starts a meeting blank.

## Before the meeting (2 minutes)

1. **Meetings → New meeting.** Title, date and attendees. Attendees matter:
   each one gets a rating row in Conclude.
2. The team's active rocks are copied onto the meeting automatically, company
   rocks first, then by owner. If a rock was added on the Rocks page after
   the meeting was created, click **Carry over active rocks** in the Rocks
   section.
3. Open the meeting. It lands on **EOS Sections**. Put it on the room screen.

## During the meeting

Work the sections top to bottom. Each one says what EOS expects of it.

**Segue (5 min).** Not on the site. Personal and business bests.

**Scorecard (5 min).** Scorecard page, in another tab. Numbers off track drop
to the Issues List; add them in the Issues section.

**Rock review (5 min).** Every rock on the list is "On Track" or "Off Track",
nothing else, no discussion. Set the status on the pill. Off track? Click
**Drop to IDS** next to it: that puts a linked issue on the Issues List, and
you move on. The status you set here is written to the rock itself, so next
week carries it over as you left it. Mark a rock "Complete" only when it is
done. A new rock needs one owner and a due date (the end of the quarter by
default) and can be marked a company or individual rock.

**Headlines (5 min).** One sentence each: customer or employee news, good or
bad, with no discussion. If it needs talking about, click **Drop to IDS** on
the headline and it becomes an issue.

**To-do list (5 min).** The section shows every open to-do of the team, not
just this meeting's. Each is "Done" or "Not Done", nothing else. Each row has
an age badge; over 14 days it turns red and the to-do should become an
issue. Closing a to-do here records this meeting as the one that closed it.
New to-dos agreed during the meeting go in with **Add to-do**: one owner,
due at the next meeting (7 days) unless you change the date.

**IDS (60 min).** The Issues section is the team's short-term Issues List in
rank order, the same list as the Issues page. Click **Pick the top 3** and
tap the three issues in the order you'll work them; they move to the top as
1, 2, 3. Then Identify, Discuss and Solve them in that order. Don't work the
list top down. An issue can be typed as a problem, information or an idea.
To mark one solved it needs a resolution note or a to-do created from it
(expand the row: type the resolution, or add the to-do with its owner).
When there is time left, pick the next three.

**Conclude (5 min).** The Conclude section at the bottom:

1. **Recap** lists the to-dos created in this meeting. Read them out.
2. **Cascading messages.** What does the rest of the firm need to hear, from
   whom, and how (Friday huddle, email, one-to-one)?
3. **Rate the meeting.** Each attendee picks 1 to 10; the average shows at the
   top right. Anything under 8: say what would make it a 10.
4. **Save conclude.**

## After the meeting

Click **Copy five lines** (top right of the page or in Conclude). It puts the
recap on the clipboard as markdown:

```
# L10 2026-10-20 (Leadership team)
Rating: 8.3 (Jude Heath: 8, Jennifer Heath: 9, Russell Heath: 8)
Rocks: 4 on track, 1 off track (Hire a senior preparer (Jennifer Heath))
To-dos: 6 done, 1 not done (Call the bank about the line (Russell Heath), 14d)
Issues: solved (Extension letters late: template fixed, Jude owns it); new (Printer in the back office)
Cascading messages: (Extension letters go out Friday, to all staff, by Jennifer Heath, via Friday huddle)
```

Paste it into chat or email. The `.md` link next to the button downloads the
same text as a file.

## If the meeting was recorded

The **Session** tab still works as before: record or upload, transcribe,
analyze, review the proposal and accept it. The analysis matches existing
rocks, to-dos and issues by id (it sees the ids in its context), updates
the rocks already on the meeting's list rather than adding duplicates, and
prefills the Conclude rating and notes. What you typed in Conclude wins
over the analysis.

## Quarter end and the Roadmap

The quarterly session is where Rocks are chosen. Rocks imported from a deck
or added to a future period on the Roadmap are **candidates** ("planned");
nothing switches them on by itself. At the session, click **Adopt as Rock**
on each one the team commits to: it needs an owner, and it gets the end of
the current quarter as its due date. The firm's quarters: Q1 May–Jul,
Q2 Aug–Oct, Q3 Nov–Jan, Q4 Feb–Apr.

Rocks → **Quarter end** shows each quarter's rocks with a Done / Not done
call per rock and the completion percentage against the 80 % target. A rock
not done is either carried into the next quarter (give it a new due date) or
dropped. The page warns, without blocking, when one person owns more than
three rocks in a quarter.

The Dashboard shows the weekly to-do drop-off: of the to-dos that were on
the list a week ago, the share that came off it. The target is about 90 %.

## The rules the site follows

- Rocks: 90-day priorities, one owner each, on track or off track only. Off
  track drops to the Issues List with no discussion. Quarter end: done or not
  done, target 80 % done. The firm's quarters run May–Jul, Aug–Oct, Nov–Jan,
  Feb–Apr.
- Headlines: one sentence, no discussion.
- To-dos: 7-day items, one owner, done or not done only. Nothing over 14
  days; about 90 % should drop off each week.
- Issues: pick the top three, solve them in order. Each solved issue ends in
  a resolution and usually a to-do.
- Conclude: recap to-dos, cascading messages, rate 1 to 10 with the average.
